Evidence and implications
-------------------------
1. AndroidAuto is an ELF32 little-endian ARM executable. DT_NEEDED includes
   libautoreceiver.so.1, libscreen.so.1 and libprotobuf-lite.so.7.
   Its strings identify AaVideoSink.cpp, dataAvailableCallback,
   sourceVideoConfigCallback, addSupportedConfigurations, MediaVideoPlayer.cpp,
   and H.264 decoding through VP_Open/VP_Decode.
   Candidate receiver hooks exist; their calling conventions are not recovered.
   Evidence: AndroidAuto.strings.txt lines 618-653.

2. libautoreceiver.so retains C++ symbol tables with VideoSink::addDiscoveryInfo,
   VideoSink::addSupportedConfiguration and protocol VideoConfiguration types.
   NavigationStatusService/InstrumentClusterInput names are also present.
   Those protocol names DO NOT establish secondary cluster VIDEO support.
   We must inspect discovery serialization and the receiver's negotiated version.

3. NavigationServer contains cluster-map activation events and the literal
   /dev/transport_server/spawn/isoTX, alongside open/close channel responses and
   start/stop-write events. This is a concrete candidate transport endpoint,
   not a license to open it or write arbitrary data to it on the car.
   Evidence: NavigationServer.strings.txt lines 4109-4118.

4. NavigationServer contains FPK encoder control diagnostics naming
   VE_InitializeProxy, VE_SetParams, VE_Open and VE_Encode, followed by a
   transportStreamPackagesReady callback and writing packages to the MOST sink.
   This strongly suggests a native map rendering -> encoder -> transport-stream
   packaging -> MOST path. Static strings alone do not recover the packet format,
   endpoint protocol, buffer ownership or active runtime configuration.
   Evidence: NavigationServer.strings.txt lines 4826-4983 and 5746-5757.

5. ClusterServices contains FB_GfxStreamSink control handlers for Connect,
   DisConnect, DataRate, RequestSync, FadeIn/FadeOut and MOST registration.
   This is evidence for a graphics-stream control plane, not the video payload
   submission API itself. Evidence: ClusterServices.strings.txt lines 420-475.

6. CarPlay imports ScreenCreate/ScreenRegister and gfxSetDisplayID.
   libScreenStream.so exposes ScreenStreamProcessData and H.264 conversion/
   decoding strings. This offers a separate CarPlay investigation path but
   does not establish support for an additional phone display.

   into the existing transport-stream/MOST path, avoiding redundant re-encoding.

been produced, and no writes to the vehicle are currently justified by triage.
